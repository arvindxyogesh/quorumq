Submit-to-ack latency runs from the start of a Submit call to the end of the
worker's successful Ack for that job: submit commit, time queued, claim commit,
worker poll delay and ack commit. Every write is committed by a Raft quorum and
fsynced before it is acknowledged.


## Network partition: 5 nodes split 2|3, old leader on the minority side

Packets between the two sides are dropped with iptables in each container's network
namespace for 30 s while load runs against all five addresses. During the
partition the script sends writes and reads straight to each minority node with
`quorumq -no-retry` (no redirects). After healing it checks that no write sent to the
minority survived, times how long the minority takes to apply everything the majority
had committed, and reconciles every acknowledged submit.
